{{ app_name }} is a study app for remembering what you learn. Recognising an answer isn't the same as knowing it, so instead of flipping cards you explain each answer in your own words and Claude grades it: how much you retained, what you missed, and a tighter way to say it. A spaced-repetition scheduler (FSRS) then brings each card back just before you'd forget it.
